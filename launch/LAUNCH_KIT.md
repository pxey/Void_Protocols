# VOID Launch Kit

A copyable starter kit for anyone who wants to start.

## Start here

Choose a place where people already exist. Create a space where people can offer useful services for free and ask for useful services for free. That is the beginning.

## Copyable community introduction

### Welcome to VOID

VOID is an open idea:

**Anyone can give. Anyone can ask. Anyone can help. Anyone can start.**

This space exists for people to offer useful services for free and ask for help they need. No payment is required. You can offer something you know how to do, ask for something you need, connect someone with a person who can help, or start your own VOID space somewhere else.

This community is independent. Take the idea, adapt it, improve it, and make it useful for your own community.

## The one-action rule

When someone discovers VOID, ask them to do one thing: give, ask, help, or start. One useful action is more valuable than passive attention.

## How to spread it

Do not spam, mass-message strangers, manufacture activity, create fake testimonials, or force referrals.

Instead, do something useful and make it easy for the next person to repeat. Sharing is optional. Replication is voluntary. No central authority is required.

## The VOID loop

```text
DISCOVER → GIVE / ASK → CONNECT → HELP → RESULT → SHARE → REPLICATE
```
