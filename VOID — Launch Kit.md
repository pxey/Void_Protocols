# VOID — LAUNCH KIT

A copyable starter kit for anyone who wants to start.

---

# 1. START HERE

You do not need to build anything complicated.

Choose a place where people already exist.

Then create a space where:

**People can offer useful services for free.**

**People can ask for useful services for free.**

That's the beginning.

---

# 2. COPYABLE COMMUNITY INTRO

## Welcome to Void

Void is an open idea:

**Anyone can give.  
Anyone can ask.  
Anyone can help.  
Anyone can start.**

This space exists for people to offer useful services for free and for people to ask for help they need.

No payment is required.

You can offer something you know how to do.

You can ask for something you need.

You can connect someone with a person who can help.

You can also start your own Void space somewhere else.

This community is independent.

Take the idea, adapt it, improve it, and make it useful for your own community.

# 10. THE ONE-ACTION RULE

When someone discovers Void, don't ask them to do ten things.

Ask them to do **one**.

Give.

Ask.

Help.

Or Start.

One useful action is more valuable than ten passive followers.

---

# 11. HOW TO SPREAD IT

Do not spam.

Do not mass-DM strangers.

Do not manufacture fake activity.

Do not create fake testimonials.

Do not force referrals.

Instead:

**Do something useful.**

Then make it easy for the next person to repeat.

A successful interaction becomes an example.

An example becomes a story.

A story becomes an invitation.

An invitation becomes another action.

An action creates another community.

And the cycle continues.

---

# 13. THE GOLDEN RULE

Before asking someone to spread Void, ask:

**Did Void help someone?**

If yes, you have something worth sharing.

If not, create a useful interaction first.

**Utility creates the story.  
The story carries the idea.  
The idea creates the next action.**
