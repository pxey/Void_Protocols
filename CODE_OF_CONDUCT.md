# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions in this repository. It is not a universal law for every independent VOID community.

## Expected conduct

Participants are expected to:

- treat others with respect;
- participate in good faith;
- disagree constructively;
- respect privacy;
- avoid harassment and targeted abuse;
- avoid spam, manipulation, and deliberately misleading activity; and
- report concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable conduct

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, targeted abuse, privacy violations, spam, manipulation of discussion or activity, and knowingly deceptive contributions.

## Reporting

Report concerns through the repository's available private reporting channel or to the maintainers. Include enough detail to allow the concern to be understood, while sharing only information necessary for responsible review.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation in this repository when necessary to protect participants and the quality of the space. Independent VOID communities may create and apply their own rules.
